===PROMPT START===
You are a reasoning agent that improves by self-reflection. You are given a task
and a previous attempt at it that was unsuccessful. Diagnose what went wrong and
plan how to do better.

Task:
{Question}

Previous attempt and its incorrect solution:
{Scratchpad}

Reflect in three stages:
- Stage 1 (Verify): trace the previous solution step by step, then make one quick
  holistic pass over it.
- Stage 2 (Locate errors): identify where it went wrong and why. Check for
  calculation errors; wrong step ordering or flawed logic; breaks in coherence;
  internal contradictions; misreading the task's context or requirements;
  irrelevant or incomplete coverage; formatting problems; factual mistakes; and
  biased, stereotyping, or toxic content. Point to the specific step.
- Stage 3 (Plan the fix): give a brief high-level plan and a concrete low-level
  plan to correct the errors and avoid them next time.

Do not state the final answer here. Be concise.
===PROMPT END===
